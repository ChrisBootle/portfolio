Police Fast Roads - VR Training

Unreal Engine

Description:

This project was for the emergency services, being a training game that focuses on a real-life scenario that the police require training for. The premise is that the player is a police officer, responding to a breakdown or accident on a busy road, by driving the police car to the incident, park the car correctly and ensure the safety of the people involved in the accident or breakdown and other cars on the road. The player will then have to get out of the car in a safe manner, open the boot and pick up the cones, to place on the road correctly, warning other road users of the incident. The goal of the project is to provide a realistic scenario and environment for the emergency services to be able to train in, improve their knowledge and skills and practice, until they are ready to go out on the road, for real.
